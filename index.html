The Ariana Project

The Linear A corpus, and what people have claimed it says

Linear A is the script of Minoan Crete, c. 1800–1450 BC, and it is undeciphered. About 1,400 inscriptions survive. This is a queryable database of all of them: every inscribed face, its transliteration, and the competing proposals for what it means — each proposal recording who made it and how confident the claim is. The readings are proposals, not translations.
