## About me

I'm Andrew, and I've always been an engineer at heart. I have a background in
chemistry, but software became my career. I'm a Senior Staff Engineer at
Coupang, and before that I spent nearly a decade at Amazon and AWS building
large-scale data systems. I helped make EMR one of the fastest Spark
distributions and designed the data platforms behind Amazon's financial
reporting. More recently, I led the architecture for AI-powered finance
automation. Outside of work, I like trying odd ideas, like letting a language
model put its own randomness into how it thinks.

On the creative side, I play piano, guitar, and drums, and I'm getting into
electronic music production. I'm also working on a large burnable temple for
Burning Man. I love Pacific Northwest nature and good mezcal and whisky, and
I've been exploring whether camas, a native PNW plant, could become a spirit
of its own.

Alongside engineering, I'm a real estate investor and short-term rental
operator based in the Seattle area. In addition to my own portfolio, I co-run
a vacation rental management company with my business partner, Zhilmil. We
handle properties for owners across the region, from modern city townhomes to
quieter retreats. Much of that work happens in spreadsheets: financial
modeling, tax strategy, and finding deals where the numbers work.

This blog is where I'll share what I'm building, learning, and wondering
about.
